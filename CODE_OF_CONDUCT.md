# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

ActionLayer is dedicated to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for all contributors, maintainers, and community members, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

---

## 2. Expected Standards

Contributors are expected to:
- Communicate with respect, empathy, and professional courtesy.
- Focus on constructive, actionable feedback during discussions and code reviews.
- Respect diverse viewpoints, technical approaches, and backgrounds.
- Gracefully accept constructive feedback and prioritize community health.

---

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:
- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or derogatory personal remarks.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or community forums.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, remarks, or physical advances.

---

## 4. Reporting Concerns & Enforcement

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it privately:

- **Email:** `rashidriyadh714@gmail.com`
- **GitHub Private Advisory:** Navigate to the repository's **Security** tab → **Advisories** → **Report a vulnerability** with the subject `[Conduct Concern]`.

*Please do not escalate conduct complaints in public issues or pull request threads.*

Maintainers will review all reports promptly and impartially. Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, reject contributions, or suspend participation for any conduct that violates these standards.
